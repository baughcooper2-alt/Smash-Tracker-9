Smash Tracker sound credits
===========================

The files in this folder were trimmed, loudness-normalized and converted to
mono MP3 for the app. The original works and their licenses are below.

Announcer voice lines (win-*, six-*, lose-*, roast-* files, except the *-joe-* ones)
  BANTER MAN, MR. HAPPY, GRENADIER, DRAGON and DROID video game announcer packs
  by VoiceBosch, part of the SoundBiter SFX library
  License: CC BY-SA 4.0  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
  https://opengameart.org/content/banter-man-video-game-announcer
  https://opengameart.org/content/mr-happy-video-game-announcer
  https://opengameart.org/content/grenadier-video-game-announcer
  https://opengameart.org/content/dragon-announcer-audio-pack
  https://opengameart.org/content/droid-video-game-announcer
  The edited versions in this folder are shared under the same license, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Crowd cheering (six-crowd-cheer.mp3, cel-crowd-roar.mp3, cel-crowd-chant.mp3)
  "Free Crowd Cheering Sounds" by Gregor Quendel
  License: CC BY 4.0  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  https://opengameart.org/content/free-crowd-cheering-sounds
  Cut to short sections with fades.

Jingles (jingle-win-*, jingle-sad-* files)
  "85 Short music jingles" by Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
  License: CC0  https://opengameart.org/content/85-short-music-jingles

Sad trombone (sad-trombone.mp3)
  "Game Over Trumpet SFX" by 0new4y
  License: CC0  https://opengameart.org/content/game-over-trumpet-sfx

Heartbeat (heartbeat.mp3)
  "Heartbeat sounds" by bart
  License: CC0  https://opengameart.org/content/heartbeat-sounds

The one-word loss (lose-fuck.mp3)
  "En-us-fuck.ogg", recorded by Benjamin Baker, 7 July 2006 (Wiktionary pronunciation)
  License: CC BY-SA 3.0  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:En-us-fuck.ogg
  Trimmed, loudness-normalized and converted to MP3; shared under CC BY-SA 3.0.

Applause (cel-applause.mp3)
  "Applause in a large hall or church" by eXpl0it3r
  License: CC0  https://opengameart.org/content/applause-in-a-large-hall-or-church

Fireworks with applause (cel-fireworks.mp3)
  "Fireworks With Applause Happy People" by Almitory (used under its CC0 option)
  License: CC0  https://opengameart.org/content/fireworks-with-applause-happy-people

Name callouts and short lines (call-*.mp3 and *-joe-*.mp3)
  Generated with the Piper text-to-speech engine (https://github.com/rhasspy/piper)
  using the "joe" voice (en_US-joe-medium), whose training data is released as CC0.
  https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US/joe/medium
All other sounds are synthesized in fx.js.
